Collection: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

Original source credits:
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/sample-source-info.txt

Original creators: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/ and
https://vis.versilstudios.com/vsco-community.html

Guitar Lab modifications: a selected subset, converted to mono MP3 at 44.1 kHz
and 96 kbps, capped at six seconds, with a 2 ms onset fade and a 150 ms fade at
the six-second cutoff. Other notes use nearby recordings with pitch shifting;
playback applies gain and release envelopes. No endorsement is implied.

`manifest.json` records each source file checksum, output checksum, pitch and
byte count. Rebuild with `python scripts/prepare-samples.py` (requires ffmpeg).
